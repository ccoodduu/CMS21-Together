## 1. Blocked flags: a remote mount added one block too many

**Symptom.** `cars.<loader>.subParts.<key>.blocked` differed, always the parts behind a wheel or hub (`s:7.7`
caliper, `s:7.8` cap, `s:7.10` stabiliser link; `s:4.*`, `s:3.*`, `s:6.*`, `s:25.0`). In every failing checkpoint
one client differs from the other three, in both directions:

| Checkpoint | Differ |
|---|---|
| 7 | C has `s:7.7/7.8/7.10` of car 2 unblocked, A, B, D blocked |
| 8 | A has `s:25.0` of car 2 unblocked, B, C, D blocked |
| 12, 13 | A unblocked, B, C, D blocked (`s:7.*` of cars 2 and 4) |
| 18 | A unblocked on car 4 (`s:4.*`), D unblocked on car 2 (`s:3.*`) |
| 20, 22–24 | B, C or D unblocked against the others |

**Cause.** `PartScript.blockedNo` is a counter, not state (native `PartScript.IsBlocked` = `blockedNo != 0`). A
mount adds one to each part in `unblockOnUnmount` (`UnblockBlockParts(false)`), an unmount takes one
(`UnblockBlockParts(true)`, clamped at 0). The actor mounts through `FastMount` → `ShowMounted` (+1). Receivers
mount through `PartApplier.Apply` (`CMS21-Together-Client/Logic/Car/Parts/PartApplier.cs:81-82` on `main`):
`ShowBySaveGame()`, which already calls `UnblockBlockParts(false)` (native `PartScript$$ShowBySaveGame.c:55`), and
then the managed `ShowMounted` replica, which called it again (`PartApplier.cs:94`). Every receiver of a mount ended
one block higher than the actor; after the next unmount the receivers kept the part blocked. The odd client in each
checkpoint is the one that mounted the blocking part last.

**Proof.** `part-blocks` (new harness verb `part-blocks` reads `blockedNo` of every part). On `main` (run
`20261008-121437_L2_part-blocks`): after A's first mount of `s:17.14` (it blocks 14 parts) the receiver B has 2 where
A has 1; after B rejoined with the part off, A has 1 where B has 0, and after B's mount A (receiver) has 3 where B
has 1. With `7e11172` (`20261008-122010_L2_part-blocks`): A and B are equal at every step and back at the spawn's
counters after each mount, including after B's rejoin.

## 2. Lifts: a car without all wheels raises the lift by itself

**Symptom.** 40 confirmed `car-placement … lift:0.state 1 vs 0` records: all four clients Middle, the server
OnFloor. Each came 6–15 s after a car was moved onto lift 1 (`Loader N moved X->3`), for example 08.23.37 → 08.23.45,
10.26.52 → 10.27.11, 11.52.15 → 11.52.22. Moves of cars with all wheels onto the same lift (loader 1 at 10.24.45,
11.26.26) gave no record. The resend then lowered a wheel-less car to the floor on every client.

**Cause.** The game raises a lift to Middle whenever it puts a car on it that misses a wheel:
`CarLoader.PlaceAtPosition` (inlined `ConnectCar`, `InstantSet(MissingWheels ? Middle : OnFloor)`,
`docs/spikes/car-placement.md`) on every client, and `NotificationCenter.<ChangeCarPos>d__20` states 4 and 6 on the
mover (also for wheels of different sizes on one axle). `CheckIfHaveWheels` reports `MissingWheels` when any part
under the four wheel holders is unmounted. Job cars come with missing parts (car 2 had its tire `s:7.28.4` off), and
the soak removes tires and rims. The server's `PlacementHandlers.OnCarPlaceChange` only knew `ResetLifterAt`
(`PlacementHandlers.cs:63,74`) and kept OnFloor.

The `placement` failures of storm 3 (K5) and checkpoints 7–8 are the same cause seen from a joining client: A
rejoined at 08.55.07 while car 2 (no tire) stood on lift 1, raised by the game on B, C, D but stored OnFloor on the
server; A applied the server's OnFloor and its lift ended disconnected (`car -1`) and "not ready" in every forced
digest (09.04.15, 09.15.35), so it was never repaired. `lift-wheels` reproduces exactly that split on `main`: after B
reconnects, A has Middle and B OnFloor.

**About `fix/lift-reset-broadcast` (`a77578a`).** Its broadcast of `ResetLifterAt`/`ResetLiftsWithoutCars` is
harmless but does not address this: when a car leaves a lift or is deleted, every client already lowers the lift
itself (`DisconnectCar`), and an empty lift cannot be raised. Its scenario passes with and without it. Recommendation:
drop it (or keep it as belt and braces; it changes nothing in this run).

**Fix (`d7f7f2f`).** The moving client computes the lift state its game ends in (`CarPlacementSync.
LiftStateAfterArrival`, the same rule as `ChangeCarPos`) for the target place and, in a swap, for its own place, and
sends it in two optional fields of `CarPlaceChangeRequest`. The server stores Middle (`PlacementRules.OnCarArrived`)
and broadcasts it. Snapshots then carry Middle to joining clients.

**Proof.** `lift-wheels`: A takes a tire off, moves the car onto lift 1, off, B moves it back, B rejoins. On `main`
(12.10): digest mismatch after both moves, and after the rejoin `Middle, OnFloor`. With `d7f7f2f` (12.14): every step
passes.

**Not covered:** a car without all wheels that arrives on a lift by spawn or unpark (no soak failure; the same field
could be added to those requests).

## 3. Drain plugs and fill caps: remote removal not applied

**Symptom.** `cars:0 … s:28.1.unmounted 0 vs 1` (B, C at 08.58.22; `power_steering_cap_1`) and `cars:4 …
s:22.1.unmounted 0 vs 1` (A, B at 09.07.41; `obudowaFiltra_gora_1`). D removed each (actions 958 and 1056) and the
server stored it; the receivers logged the visual ("Off started on loader 0 s:28.1") but kept the part on.

**Cause.** Native `PartScript.HideBySavegame` returns at once for special group 1 (`OilDrainCheckFill`: drain plugs,
oil, power steering and filter housing caps, dipsticks; `PartScript$$HideBySavegame.c:60`). `PartApplier.Apply`
(`PartApplier.cs:77` on `main`) used it for every remote unmount and reported success.

**Fix (`48f0420`).** `PartApplier` clears the special group for the one `HideBySavegame` call and restores it.

**Proof.** `part-drain-cap`: A removes `korek_spustowy_1` (oil drain plug), B rejoins, A puts it back. On `main`
(12.19): B keeps it on, also after the rejoin, digest mismatch. With `48f0420` (12.24): passes.

## 4. TCP framing: a length prefix split across two reads

**Symptom.** Storm 9 (K8): D rejoined at 11:09:36, received the world and garage state, then logged `Error reading
packet 4782080: Offset and length were out of bounds` (11:09:40.038), never got the remaining car-placement, tools,
jobs and `SyncEnd`, and failed after 30 s ("Loading the shared garage failed"). Rule 3 (D could not rejoin), rule 4
(the two errors) and rule 5 (storm 9).

**Cause.** `ClientTCP.HandleData` and the server's `Tcp.HandleData` (the tutorial framing,
`CMS21-Together-Client/Network/Transport/ClientTCP.cs:92-133`, `CMS21-Together-Server/Network/Transport/TCP.cs:62-108`
on `main`) read the 4-byte length only when four bytes are left; with one to three left they return `true`, which
clears the buffer. The next read then starts inside a packet: garbage ids (4782080) and lost packets until the stream
happens to realign. A snapshot is about 600 kB, so the chance grows with every join.

**Fix (`cec6c31`).** `PacketFramer` (Core) used by both receivers keeps every incomplete packet, prefix included, and
compacts its buffer.

**Proof.** `CMS21_Together_Server.exe --check-framing`: 300 packets cut at 1, 3, 4096, 4097 and random bytes and
inside every length prefix. The old logic (ported into `PacketFramer` for the run): 1-byte 0/300, 3-byte 0/300,
inside-prefix 1/300 packets. The new framer: all six pass. (The storm itself cannot be forced to split a prefix.)

Also: the storm's last message "after K8: You cannot call a method on a null-valued expression" was the harness
throwing on D's missing dump section; `bc8b331` prints it instead.

## 5. Inventory change during a scene load

**Symptom.** D, rule 4: `Error in handler InventoryGroupItemAction: Object reference not set` (09:48:01.944) and
`… InventoryItemAction …` (11:20:25.956), both while D was in `SceneLoader` on the way to the junkyard.

**Cause.** `GameManager` has no inventory while a scene loads. `InventoryHandlers.HandleInventoryItemAction` and
`HandleInventoryGroupItemAction` (`CMS21-Together-Client/Network/Handlers/InventoryHandlers.cs:165-221` on `main`)
dereference `Singleton<GameManager>.Instance.Inventory` directly; the exception comes from the handler frame itself,
and the change is lost on that client. Only the full inventory sync was held (`HoldDuringFullSync`).

**Fix (`296a010`).** The hold (now `HoldUntilReady`) also holds inventory, warehouse, shop and part-delta changes
while the inventory or warehouse is missing, keeps their order and replays them once both exist.

**Proof.** `inventory-travel`: A travels to the junkyard and back while B adds an item or a group every 150 ms. On
`main` (12.25): 14 handler errors on A. With `296a010` (12.29): no error, A's inventory equals B's.

## 6. Server memory (rule 7) and the budget warnings

**Rule 7 (server private end / minute 10 = 1.53×).** Not a leak.

- The server ran as six processes: storms 1, 2, 4, 5 and 9 (K8) restart it. The minute-10 value (71.6 MB) is from the
  first process, the end value (109.6 MB) from the sixth.
- Each new process starts higher because it loads a larger save: private bytes 10 s after start 54, 86, 91, 100, 100,
  114 MB, while the save grew from 9 kB to 2.97 MB at about 70 kB per 10 minutes (`perf_server.jsonl`).
- Within a process the managed heap after collections stays flat (13–25 MB from 08.27 to 11.58); private bytes follow
  the heap's peaks (up to 79 MB) during save and snapshot building.
- The save grows because the soak's workload grows: the shared inventory went from 0 to 932 items (checkpoints),
  647 kB of the save; 812 unmounts against 734 mounts and 135 gives.

Proposal (not done, needs your decision): measure rule 7 per server process (from the uptime column) or against the
save size, and let the soak keep the inventory bounded (sell or scrap down to a ceiling).

**Budgets.** "Handler max 111 ms" is `save-build`, and "lock wait 100 ms" is a `StateDigest` waiting for it:
`GameDataManager.SaveSession` builds the whole envelope under `StateLock` (`GameDataManager.cs:163-174`), 27 ms at
the start and 110–170 ms with the 2.9 MB save. Possible fix: cache the JSON of sections that did not change. The game
private-bytes slope (B 235, D 280 MB/h; A and C negative after their K4 restart) is the game process, likely the same
growing inventory; not analysed further.
